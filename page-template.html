About the Workshop

Bridging AI and Industrial IoT Security

An established international forum at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Industrial IoT, Cyber-Physical Systems, and cybersecurity — now in its 9th edition.

9th Edition
Running since 2019
Double-Blind
Anonymous peer review
Springer LNCS
Indexed proceedings
18 + 12 pp
Main + Appendix limit

AIoTS explores two complementary directions: leveraging AI and machine learning to advance security solutions for IIoT/CPS environments, and applying cybersecurity principles to protect AI algorithms from adversarial threats. The workshop brings together leading researchers and practitioners to share cutting-edge findings and shape the trajectory of secure AI-driven industrial systems.

AIoTS 2027 is co-located with ACNS 2027, the 25th International Conference on Applied Cryptography and Network Security, hosted at Royal Holloway, University of London, Egham, UK. Accepted papers will appear in ACNS 2027 satellite workshop proceedings published by Springer LNCS.

Research Scope

Topics of Interest

Original research contributions on (but not limited to) the following topics:

Formal security and resilience analysis for AI and IIoT/CPS
Risk management and governance for AI and IIoT applications
AI-assisted critical infrastructure security
Federated and adversarial machine learning for security
AI for detection, prevention, response and recovery from cyber threats
AI for wide-area situational awareness and traceability
Applied cryptography for AI and IIoT environments
Security and privacy of Cyber-Physical Systems and/or IIoT
Applications of formal methods to IIoT security
Blockchain for trustworthy IIoT/CPS based applications
Embedded systems and firmware security
Privacy-preserving machine learning techniques
Cyber threat intelligence for AI and IIoT/CPS
Security of agentic and autonomous AI systems in industrial settings: excessive agency,scope enforcement, and execution-layer containment
Security of large language models and foundation models in IIoT/CPS operations
Assurance, certification, and evidence for AI-enabled industrial systems

AI-assisted adversary emulation, red teaming, and attack generation
Digital and cyber twins for security evaluation of industrial systems
Safety and security co-engineering for cyber-physical and robotic systems
